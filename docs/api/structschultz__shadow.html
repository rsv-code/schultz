Detailed Description

A shadow cast by a node and everything under it.

The whole subtree is drawn once, and that picture is what casts the shadow, so a card with a title and a button on it casts one shadow in the shape of the card rather than three overlapping ones.

◆ angle

float schultz_shadow::angle

Which way it falls, in degrees clockwise, with 0 directly above the node. So 90 is to its right, 180 below it, and 270 to its left. Below is the usual answer, which makes 180 the usual angle.

Clockwise, and measured from above, to agree with the rotation a draw list can push. One meaning for degrees across the toolkit.

A node that is itself turned keeps its shadow falling the same way on screen, because a light source does not turn with the thing it lights.

◆ blur

float schultz_shadow::blur

How soft the edge is. Zero gives a hard edged copy of the shape, which is a real shadow and not a special case. Larger spreads it further, and spreading costs blur work over a larger area.

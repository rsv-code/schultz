Detailed Description

How hard each of the four pushes.

The switches above say whether something happens; this says how much. The defaults are speexdsp's own, are what most callers should leave alone, and are what schultz_voice_tuning_default fills in.

The two units are decibels and a fraction of full scale, and neither is a knob to turn blind: -15 dB of noise removal is gentle and -30 is heavy enough to hear the voice being chewed as well.

float schultz_voice_tuning::echo_removal_db

Most the residual echo is pushed down by, in decibels, negative. Default -40. This is what is left after the canceller has done its work, so it is a safety net rather than the main mechanism.

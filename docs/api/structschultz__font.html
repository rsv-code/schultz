◆ family

char schultz_font::family[64]

The family this face belongs to, copied from the face when it loads. A Bold button sets bold on a span rather than naming a handle, and the family is how the bold member of that span's face is found.

Empty for a face that names no family. Such a face joins no family and is only ever itself, which is the right answer: guessing that two unnamed faces are related would substitute the wrong glyphs.
